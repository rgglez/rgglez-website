On March 31, 2026, Instagram surprised us with an unexpected announcement:
Jean-Michel Jarre, the legendary French musician, was about to release a new
book titled _Machines_. The post by Sonny Marr (of Marr Literary), the literary
agent initially involved, can be seen on her Instagram account, where she
enthusiastically shared the news. She was invited to develop the project by
Alistair Norbury, a senior executive at BMG (where he is President of Repertoire
& Marketing for the UK, Europe and APAC).

eventually join Marjacq Scripts, and the book rights would be acquired by
Thames & Hudson through Tristan de Lancey (publisher and creative director).

Jean-Michel Jarre's book *Machines: A History of Electronic Music* (in French:
*Machines : Histoire de la musique électronique*) was officially announced in
late March/early April 2026 by Thames & Hudson (with the French edition
published by Éditions du Chêne). Its publication date is October 8, 2026
(UK/international), and around October 14 for the French edition.
<Image
  src={book02}
  alt="Inside pages of Machines by Jean-Michel Jarre"
  class="float-right mb-4 ml-6 h-auto w-2/5 max-w-50"
/>
It is a large illustrated book (368 pages) centered on Jarre's personal
collection of more than 80 electronic instruments, with contributions from Pete
Townshend, Armin van Buuren, Gary Numan, Gillian Gilbert, among others.
